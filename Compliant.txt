1. Toilet Soap (BIS IS 2888:2004)
To achieve a "Grade 1" classification with zero critical violations:
Total Fatty Matter (TFM): 78% – 82% (Must comfortably clear the 76% minimum)
Moisture Content: 10% – 12% (Easily under the 15% maximum limit)
Glycerin (Humectant): 2% – 5%
Sodium Cocoate / Sodium Laureth Sulfate (Surfactants): 10% – 15%
Sodium Hydroxide (Lye): 0.01% – 0.04% (Must stay strictly below the 0.05% burn threshold)
Sodium Chloride (Salt): < 0.5%
Fragrance & Chelating Agents (EDTA): < 0.1% each.

2. Talcum Powder (BIS IS 1462:2019)
A perfectly compliant, baby-safe/cosmetic talc formulation:
Cosmetic Talc (Hydrated Magnesium Silicate): 95% – 98%
Magnesium Carbonate / Zinc Oxide (Absorbents): 1% – 3%
Fragrance Mixture: 0.5%
Methylparaben (Preservative): 0.1% – 0.2%
Contaminants:
Asbestos: Strictly 0% (Banned)
Crystalline Silica: < 0.1% (Must be under 1.0%)

3. Hair Oil (BIS IS 7123:2019)
Whether formulating Type 1 (Vegetable) or Type 3 (Blended) oils:
Base Oil (Coconut Oil / Mineral Oil / Olive Oil): 90% – 95%
Castor Oil / Almond Oil (Secondary Emollients): 3% – 5%
Herbal Extracts (e.g., Aloe Vera): 1% – 2%
Acid Value: < 0.5% (Must stay below 1.0 to prevent scalp severe irritation and signal pure stability)
Moisture: < 1.0% (Must safely clear the 5.5% boundary to prevent rapid spoilage)
Heavy Metals (Lead, Arsenic, Mercury): 0 ppm limit detected in raw sourcing.

4. Moisturizing Cream / Lotion (BIS IS 6608:2004)
A stable, skin-barrier protective lotion formulation:
Water (Aqua): 70% – 80% (Primary solvent)
Glycerin (Humectant): 5% – 10%
Stearic Acid & Cetyl Alcohol (Emollients/Thickeners): 3% – 6%
Shea Butter / Olive Oil: 2% – 5%
pH Adjusters (like Citric Acid): Added in trace amounts to stabilize the final product pH between 5.5 and 6.5 (Safely within the 4.0 - 9.0 legal mandate).
Methylparaben / Butylparaben (Preservatives): 0.2% (Safely below the 0.4% cosmetic max threshold)

5. Body Spray / Deodorant / Perfume (BIS IS 8482:2007)
A standard, non-toxic aerosol compilation:
Ethyl Alcohol (Ethanol / Denatured Alcohol): 45% – 60%
Butane / Isobutane Propellant: 30% – 40%
Aluminum Chlorohydrate (Antiperspirant Active): 10% – 18% (Under the typical 25% max limit)
Triclosan (Antibacterial Agent): 0.1% – 0.2% (Clear of the 0.3% warning mark)
Fragrance (Linalool / Limonene): 1% – 3%
Impurities Profile:
Methanol: < 0.05% (Safely under the 0.2% toxicity limit from raw alcohol)
Benzene: 0% (Banned propellant impurity)
